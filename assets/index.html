The book

A doorway story built like an evidence file.

Ghosts in the Code begins with a death event and follows the consequences into near-death experience, paranormal field work, evidential mediumship, and the ethical shock of AI recreating the dead before anyone has settled what “contact” even means.

Core promise

The dead do not need our devices. We do.

The site keeps that promise visible: cinematic enough to feel like the book, simple enough that readers, podcasters, producers, and reviewers can understand it in seconds.
